# Area: pricing, testimonials and social proof

Use when the scope is a pricing section, plan comparison, testimonials, customer logos, case-study strip, FAQ or a trust bar. These sections are where invented content creeps in most, so truth comes before design.

## Pricing

- Only plans, prices and features the client has confirmed. If prices are unknown, show "Contact us" or a clearly marked placeholder, and list it in the report.
- Currency and format follow the audience (see `india.md`). State whether GST is included, once, near the price.
- Compare plans in a table or aligned columns with the same rows in the same order. Do not show a "most popular" badge unless the client has data that supports it. The recommended plan is marked with a quiet label and one surface step, not a glow or a scaled-up card.
- One primary button per plan, same wording style ("Choose Starter"), no exclamation marks.
- The billing toggle shows the real saving and what it compares to ("Save ₹2,400 a year").
- Features are described by what the customer gets, not by internal names. Three to seven rows per plan; beyond that, group them.
- Explain the one thing people ask before buying (refunds, cancellation, what happens after the trial) in a short line beneath the plans.

## Testimonials

- Only real quotes from real customers with their permission. A name, a role or business, and ideally a link or photo they supplied.
- If there are none yet, leave the section out or mark a visible placeholder in design files and list it in the report. Do not write quotes, do not use stock faces, do not use `i.pravatar.cc` or similar.
- One strong quote with a specific result reads better than a grid of six vague ones ("great service!"). Prefer quotes that name what changed.
- Set quotes in the body face at a larger size. No giant decorative quotation marks, no five-star row unless it links to a real review source.
- Real review platforms (Google reviews, Play Store) can be linked or quoted with the source shown.

## Logos and counts

- Customer logos only with permission. A row of logos the client does not have is a lie.
- Counts ("200+ shops") only when the client confirms the number and the date. State the date or the source. Round down, not up.
- Replace vague counts with something checkable: a list of named clients, a map of locations, a live link.

## Case-study strip

Link to the project page. Show a real screenshot, the client or kind of client, one result with its source. See `skills-proof.md` for evidence rules.

## FAQ

- Real questions that customers asked. Not generated filler.
- Accordion with native `details`/`summary` or a button pattern with correct ARIA, and a visible focus ring.
- Answers short, specific, no marketing language.
- Use FAQ structured data only on pages with real questions and answers.

## Layout and detail

- Vary the rhythm: one wide proof element (the best quote or one case study) plus a small supporting list, not a grid of equal boxes.
- Quiet chrome: hairline borders, surface steps, neutral text. The accent marks the recommended plan or the primary button only.
- Numbers (prices, counts) use tabular figures and one format.

## Checks for this area

- Every price, plan, name, quote, logo and number has a source the client confirmed, or is removed or marked and listed.
- No stock faces beside testimonials. No invented avatars.
- No glowing "popular" plan, no scaled hover card.
- Plans compare on identical rows. Currency and GST format are right for the market.
- The section passes the template test.
